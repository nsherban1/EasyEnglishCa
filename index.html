Built from years of real classroom experience.

Easy English Canada was created by an experienced ESL teacher with more than nine years of teaching experience.

After four years teaching English in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and five years teaching students online, our approach has been shaped by working with learners from different ages, backgrounds and proficiency levels.
